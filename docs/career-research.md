## Research Question

What skills, education, and experience do employers look for in careers involving artificial intelligence, software implementation, and data analytics? How can I prepare for these careers through my Business and Information Systems education and project experience?

## Career Direction 1: AI and Software Implementation

This career direction focuses on using technology and artificial intelligence to solve problems for businesses. Professionals in these roles help build, connect, test, and implement software systems.

### Employer 1: Wipro

**Position:** Junior Forward Deployed Engineer  
**Location:** East Brunswick, New Jersey, among other listed locations  
**Posting date:** October 26, 2026  
**Experience required:** 0–2 years

**Main responsibilities:**
- Help businesses deploy and configure AI software.
- Connect AI applications to business systems and data.
- Test software and troubleshoot technical problems.
- Help integrate AI applications, including large language model applications.
- Communicate with customers and explain technical information.
- Create documentation and help improve software solutions.

**Required qualifications:**
- Bachelor's or master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field.
- Minimum 3.0 GPA on a 4.0 scale.
- 0–2 years of relevant experience.
- Interest in customer-facing engineering and applied technology.

**Skills mentioned:**
- Programming and software engineering.
- AI applications and large language models.
- Data systems and integrations.
- Technical problem-solving.
- Communication and teamwork.

**My comparison:**

This is the most realistic early-career example among the three positions researched. The experience requirement is relatively low, and relevant internships or experience are considered a plus. However, the degree requirement is more technical than my current Business and Information Systems major. I would need to strengthen my programming and technical skills.

**Official employer source:**  
https://careers.wipro.com/job/Junior-Forward-Deployed-Engineer/160017-en_US

## Career Direction 2: AI Engineering, Data Analytics, and Business Operations

This career direction focuses on using data, software, and AI to improve business decisions and operations. These skills can be used in many industries, including consumer products, healthcare, and supply chain management.

### Employer 2: Colgate-Palmolive

**Position:** AI Solution Engineer  
**Location:** Piscataway, New Jersey  
**Posting date:** November 2, 2026  
**Experience required:** 4+ years of professional software engineering experience

**Main responsibilities:**
- Build and support business applications that use AI.
- Develop software that uses large language models and AI agents.
- Connect applications to databases and business systems.
- Build front-end and back-end software.
- Test applications and monitor their performance.
- Work with business teams to understand problems and develop solutions.

**Required qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
- At least four years of professional software engineering experience.
- Experience developing and supporting full-stack applications.

**Other important skills:**
- Python or Node.js.
- JavaScript and React.
- SQL and APIs.
- Cloud services and databases.
- AI tools, including prompt engineering and retrieval-augmented generation.
- Software testing and security.

**My comparison:**

This position is interesting because Information Systems is included among the accepted degree fields. It combines business needs with programming and AI development. However, the four-year professional experience requirement makes this a longer-term career goal rather than an immediate opportunity.

**Official employer source:**  
https://jobs.colgate.com/job/AI-Solution-Engineer/176868-en_US/

## Comparison of the Two Career Directions

| Category | AI and Software Implementation | Data Analytics and Supply Chain |
|---|---|---|
| Main focus | Building and implementing technology | Improving business operations using data |
| Important skills | Programming, AI, APIs, testing | SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI |
| Business involvement | Working with customers and technical teams | Working with business and operations teams |
| Example employer | Wipro and Colgate-Palmolive | Johnson & Johnson |
| Potential career growth | FDE, AI Engineer, Solutions Architect | Data Analyst, Supply Chain Analyst, Analytics Manager |

Both career directions involve technology and problem-solving. The main difference is how the technology is used. AI implementation focuses more on developing and deploying software, while data analytics focuses more on understanding information and improving business decisions.
